# Data Deletion & Subject Access Requests — Pulse

## How to request deletion of your data
1. In-app: Account Settings → Privacy → “Delete my data / Close account”
2. Email: privacy@[yourdomain] with subject “Data Deletion Request”
3. Include the email associated with the account

## What we delete
- Account profile
- Projects and generated code stored on our servers (self-hosted instances are under your control)
- Logs that can be linked to you
- Billing records are retained only as required by tax/law

## Timeline
We aim to complete deletion within 30 days (often faster). You will receive confirmation.

## Exceptions
- Data we must keep for legal, tax, or security reasons (will be isolated and not used for other purposes)
- Data already anonymised

## Access / Portability
You can also request a copy of your data (export) via the same channels.
