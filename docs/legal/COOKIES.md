# Cookie Policy — Pulse

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## What are cookies
Small files stored on your device that help the site work and remember preferences.

## How we use cookies

| Category | Purpose | Required? |
|----------|---------|-----------|
| Strictly necessary | Authentication, security, load balancing, cookie consent itself | Yes |
| Preferences | Theme, language, UI settings | No (consent) |
| Analytics | Understand usage (anonymised where possible) | No (consent) |
| Marketing | Only if we ever run ads — currently none planned | No (consent) |

## Consent
We show a clear cookie consent banner on first visit.  
- Equal weight for “Accept all”, “Reject non-essential”, and “Customize”
- No pre-ticked boxes for non-essential cookies
- You can change your mind anytime via the cookie settings link in the footer

We use open-source consent tools (e.g. c15t or equivalent) so consent records stay under our control where possible.

## Third-party
Any third-party scripts (analytics, payment, etc.) are loaded only after appropriate consent.  
We audit SDKs regularly and remove unnecessary ones.

## Your choices
Browser settings + our consent banner + account settings.
