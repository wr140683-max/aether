## 2. What data we collect
We follow **data minimisation**. We only collect what is necessary.

| Data | Why | Legal basis |
|------|-----|-------------|
| Account email + name | Account creation, communication | Contract / Legitimate interest |
| Project files & prompts you submit | To generate and improve your apps | Contract |
| Usage logs (anonymised) | Improve product, security | Legitimate interest |
| Payment info | Billing (processed by Stripe / your processor) | Contract |
| Cookie preferences | Consent management | Consent |

**We do NOT collect:**
- Unnecessary personal data
- Data about children under 13/16 without verified parental consent
- Sensitive categories unless you explicitly provide them in your projects

## 3. How we use data
- Provide and improve the service
- Communicate about your account / builds
- Security, fraud prevention, legal compliance
- Never sell your personal data

## 4. Third-party services
We only use essential processors (hosting, payments, email). Full list will be maintained in the Cookie Policy / Subprocessors page. All are under data processing agreements.

## 5. Your rights
- Access, rectification, erasure, restriction, portability, objection
- **Data deletion request**: Email privacy@... or use the in-app “Delete my data” option. We respond within 30 days.
- Withdraw consent at any time (cookie banner + account settings)
- Unsubscribe from marketing emails (one-click in every email)

## 6. Children
Pulse is not directed at children under 13 (or 16 where required). We do not knowingly collect their data. If we learn we have, we delete it.

## 7. Retention
Account data: while account is active + reasonable period after.  
Logs: typically 90 days or less (anonymised where possible).  
You can request earlier deletion.

## 8. International transfers
If data leaves your region, we use appropriate safeguards (SCCs, etc.).
