# Refund Policy — Pulse

**Last updated:** October 27, 2026

## Our commitment
We believe in fair treatment. No dark patterns around money.

## Subscriptions
- You can cancel anytime. Access continues until the end of the current billing period.
- Unused prepaid periods are generally non-refundable except where required by law (e.g. EU 14-day cooling-off for consumers) or as stated below.

## Credits / Usage-based charges
- Credits that were not used due to a clear platform error (agent loop we caused, prolonged outage, etc.) may be restored or refunded at our discretion.
- Normal usage, including agent iterations you requested, is not refundable.
- We will never charge for “thinking time” in a hidden way — all consumption is visible.

## How to request
Email support@[yourdomain] with:
- Account email
- Reason
- Relevant project / transaction IDs

We aim to respond within 5 business days.

## Chargebacks
Please contact us first. Unwarranted chargebacks may result in account suspension.

## Local consumer law
Nothing in this policy limits your statutory rights.
